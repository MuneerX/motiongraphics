# Luxury Dark 3D Tech Launch Look

Dark obsidian enterprise glassmorphism, Saudi Fab yellow (`#FEEC3C`), industrial steel navy (`#0058A3`), and MubarakTech cyan (`#38BDF8`) accents.
Engineered for digital product launch videos, B2B platforms, SaaS reveals, and enterprise engineering showcases.

## Palette
- Ground: `#050811` (obsidian deep space)
- Glass Card: `rgba(13, 21, 39, 0.85)` with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- Accent Primary: `#FEEC3C` (Saudi Fab Store Industrial Gold/Yellow)
- Accent Secondary: `#38BDF8` (MubarakTech Electric Cyan)
- Tech Blue: `#0058A3` (Saudi Industrial Standard Blue)
- Text: `#FFFFFF` (Heading), `#E2E8F0` (Body), `#94A3B8` (Muted)

## Components
- `deviceMockup(x, y, w, h, assetName, o)`: Browser/chassis housing real web screenshots with URL bar.
- `techCard(x, y, w, h, o)`: Glassmorphic container with rounded borders and glow.
- `techBadge(str, x, y, o)`: Pill tag with glowing LED indicator.
- `metricCounter(numStr, label, x, y, w, h, o)`: Animated KPI telemetry card.
- `drawHeroSpark(x, y, r, o)`: Rotating 3D gyroscopic precision atom hero.
- `drawAgencySignature(cx, cy, scale, o)`: MubarakTech signature lockup with anamorphic light flare.
